A field guide to the natural world

Discover the little wonders of wildlife

Explore butterflies, beetles, spiders, carnivorous plants, ants, and snakes. Meet remarkable species and learn what makes each one unique.

Butterflies

Monarch butterfly

Danaus plexippus ? famous for its long migration.

Peacock butterfly

Aglais io ? recognized by its vivid eye-spots.

Brimstone butterfly

Gonepteryx rhamni ? a bright yellow European butterfly.

Swallowtail butterfly

Papilio machaon ? a large butterfly with tail-like wings.

Cassius blue

Leptotes cassius ? a tiny butterfly in the blues family.

African charaxes

Charaxes brutus natalensis ? a fast-flying African species.

Red admiral

Vanessa atalanta - a striking migratory butterfly.

Painted lady

Vanessa cardui - found across a remarkably wide range.

Blue morpho

Morpho peleides - known for brilliant iridescent blue wings.

Zebra longwing

Heliconius charithonia - Florida state butterfly.

Orange-tip

Anthocharis cardamines - males have bright orange wing tips.

Common buckeye

Junonia coenia - recognizable by its large eyespots.

Beetles

Stag beetle

Lucanus cervus ? males have enlarged antler-like jaws.

Hercules beetle

Dynastes hercules ? one of the largest beetles.

Dung beetle

Scarabaeus viettei ? helps recycle nutrients in ecosystems.

Bombardier beetle

Brachinus species ? known for its defensive spray.

Colorado potato beetle

Leptinotarsa decemlineata ? a striped leaf beetle.

Japanese beetle

Popillia japonica ? metallic green and copper in color.

Goliath beetle

Goliathus goliatus - one of the heaviest beetles.

Seven-spot ladybird

Coccinella septempunctata - a familiar garden beetle.

Golden tortoise beetle

Charidotella sexpunctata - its shell can appear metallic gold.

Jewel beetle

Chrysochroa fulminans - a brilliantly colored jewel beetle.

Firefly

Photinus pyralis - a bioluminescent beetle.

Eastern Hercules beetle

Dynastes tityus - a North American rhinoceros beetle.

Spiders

Redback spider

Latrodectus hasselti ? an Australian spider in the widow family.

Goliath birdeater

Theraphosa blondi ? a large South American tarantula.

Jumping spider

Platycryptus undatus ? an agile hunter with excellent vision.

Cobalt blue tarantula

Haplopelma lividum ? a vivid blue Southeast Asian tarantula.

Orb-weaver spider

Araneus diadematus ? builds a distinctive circular web.

Wolf spider

Lycosidae ? a ground-hunting spider that does not rely on a web to catch prey.

Carnivorous plants

Venus flytrap

Dionaea muscipula ? closes its leaves around small prey.

Cape sundew

Drosera capensis ? catches insects on sticky leaf tentacles.

Round-leaved sundew

Drosera rotundifolia ? a small bog plant with glistening traps.

Sarracenia pitcher plant

Sarracenia ? uses pitcher-shaped leaves to trap insects.

Nepenthes rajah

Nepenthes rajah ? a tropical pitcher plant from Borneo.

Cobra lily

Darlingtonia californica ? its hooded leaves resemble a cobra.

Common butterwort

Pinguicula vulgaris ? captures tiny insects on sticky leaves.

Ants

Leafcutter ant

Atta cephalotes ? carries leaf pieces to grow its food fungus.

Carpenter ant

Camponotus species ? nests in wood rather than eating it.

Red weaver ant

Oecophylla smaragdina ? joins leaves to build its nest.

Fire ant

Solenopsis species ? forms large, organized colonies.

Black garden ant

Lasius niger ? a familiar ant across much of Europe.

Pharaoh ant

Monomorium pharaonis ? a tiny ant with a widespread distribution.

Army ant

Dorylus species ? travels in large, coordinated groups.

Argentine ant

Linepithema humile - forms extensive cooperative colonies.

Bullet ant

Paraponera clavata - a large ant of Central and South American forests.

Trap-jaw ant

Odontomachus - snaps its jaws shut at extraordinary speed.

Honeypot ant

Myrmecocystus - some workers store liquid food for the colony.

Red harvester ant

Pogonomyrmex barbatus - collects and stores seeds.

Acrobat ant

Crematogaster - raises its abdomen when disturbed.

Snakes

King cobra

Ophiophagus hannah ? the world?s longest venomous snake.

Ball python

Python regius ? curls into a ball when feeling threatened.

Green tree python

Morelia viridis ? rests coiled among branches.

Corn snake

Pantherophis guttatus ? a nonvenomous North American snake.

Reticulated python

Malayopython reticulatus ? named for its intricate pattern.

Western hognose snake

Heterodon nasicus ? known for its upturned snout.

Gaboon viper

Bitis gabonica ? a forest viper with remarkable camouflage.
